Shukra Pradosh Vrat on 23 October 2026: Shiva-Shakti Worship, Puja Vidhi and Astrological Significance

Shukra Pradosh Vrat will be observed on Friday, 23 October 2026, on Trayodashi Tithi of the Shukla Paksha. Know its significance, puja vidhi, simple fasting rules and useful self-reflection guidance for each zodiac sign.

Shukra Pradosh Vrat on 23 October 2026

In India, Pradosh Vrat will be observed on Friday, 23 October 2026, on Trayodashi Tithi of the Shukla Paksha. Since it falls on a Friday, it is known as Shukra Pradosh Vrat. Pradosh Vrat is associated with the worship of Lord Shiva, while Friday is traditionally associated with the planet Venus in astrology. Therefore, this day may be considered suitable for reflecting on marital harmony, sweetness in relationships, appreciation of beauty, creativity and balance in life.

According to the available Panchang details for New Delhi, the Pradosh puja period on 23 October will be from 5:44 p.m. to 8:16 p.m. However, sunrise, sunset and the calculation of the Tithi may vary from one city to another. Before taking a vow to observe the puja or fast, be sure to check the timing in your local Panchang.

The purpose of this day is not only to seek the fulfilment of a particular wish, but also to practise discipline, restraint, gratitude and mental peace. Along with worship, humility in conduct, open communication within the family and helping someone in need can also be connected with the spirit of this vrat.

Religious and Astrological Significance of Pradosh Vrat

Pradosh refers to the evening period regarded as the transition between day and night. Traditionally, this time is considered special for remembering Lord Shiva, lighting a lamp, chanting mantras and calming the mind. During the Pradosh period of Trayodashi Tithi, Shiva and Parvati are worshipped, and many devotees also seek darshan of Nandi and perform abhishek.

This Pradosh Vrat, associated with Friday, offers a special opportunity to focus on matters connected with relationships and lifestyle. From an astrological perspective, Venus is regarded as the significator of love, marriage, art, music, beauty, material comforts and diplomatic conduct. This does not mean that observing a fast for one day will certainly bring marriage, wealth or success. Nevertheless, the day can be useful for reviewing one’s conduct, spending habits and balance in relationships.

Simple Puja Vidhi for Shukra Pradosh Vrat

1. Morning Sankalp and Purity

Take a bath in the morning and wear clean clothes. Clean the place of worship and remember Lord Shiva, Goddess Parvati and Nandi. Take the sankalp to observe the fast according to your capacity. If fasting is difficult because of health, age, pregnancy, medication or any other reason, choosing fruit-based food or a sattvic diet is more appropriate.

2. Worship During the Pradosh Period

During the evening Pradosh period, offer clean water to the Shivling. If available, milk, curd, honey or Gangajal may be used in limited quantities, but offering water alone is sufficient. Offer bel leaves, akshat, white flowers, incense and a lamp. When offering bel leaves, ensure that they are clean and intact. Avoid unnecessary wastage of religious materials.

3. Mantra and Prayer

Chant the mantra Om Namah Shivaya with a calm mind. After this, you may recite the Shiva Chalisa, Rudrashtakam or a simple hymn according to your tradition. Since it is Friday, Goddess Parvati and the nurturing aspect associated with Lakshmi may also be remembered, while Lord Shiva remains the central focus of the worship.

4. Naivedya and Service

Offer fruit, makhana, kheer or any other sattvic prasad. After the puja, share the prasad with family members. Offering food, clothing or assistance according to your capacity to someone in need connects the spiritual feeling of the day with practical action. In the evening, make an effort to avoid anger, harsh words and unnecessary arguments.

Things to Keep in Mind During the Fast

  • Observe a waterless fast only if your health permits it and you are already accustomed to this type of practice.
  • For a fruit-based fast, fresh fruit, milk, curd, makhana, sabudana or light sattvic food may be taken. Give priority to your personal dietary tradition.
  • People with diabetes, blood pressure, kidney, stomach or other health conditions should not fast without medical advice.
  • Do not offer anything to the Shivling that is considered prohibited according to the customs of your local temple or family tradition.
  • Rather than imposing the fast on others, adopt it as a personal spiritual practice and exercise in self-discipline.

People belonging to Taurus and Libra may focus on maintaining balance between expenses, relationships and comforts. Gemini and Virgo natives should avoid rushing in conversations and using overly critical language. Cancer and Pisces natives can practise expressing their emotional boundaries and personal needs clearly. For Leo and Aries, giving greater priority to cooperation than to ego may prove useful.

Scorpio and Sagittarius natives can take steps towards resolving an old disagreement peacefully. Practical Guidance for Marriage, Love and Career

For married people and those in romantic relationships, this day can become an opportunity for open communication rather than making a list of complaints. Listening to one’s partner without interrupting, helping with household responsibilities and maintaining transparency in financial decisions are practical expressions of positive Venusian qualities. Unmarried people should focus on respect, trust and shared values in any relationship, rather than attraction alone.

In the career sphere, Shukra Pradosh may offer people working in creative fields, design, art, media, fashion, hospitality, diplomacy and customer relations an opportunity to review their working style. Do not make decisions about an investment, job change or partnership solely on the basis of a fast or an astrological indication. Give priority to facts, contracts, your financial position and expert advice.

Three Common Questions

Is it compulsory to observe Pradosh Vrat on 23 October 2026?

No. The fast should be observed voluntarily and according to one’s physical capacity. If fasting is not possible, simple practices such as bathing, lighting a lamp, chanting a Shiva mantra, eating sattvic food and offering service may be followed.

Will Shukra Pradosh Vrat definitely improve marriage or love relationships?

No such guarantee can be given. This vrat is a means of religious faith and self-discipline. Respect, clear communication, trust and, when needed, counselling are also important for improving relationships.

Will the Pradosh puja time be the same in every city?

No. The Pradosh period depends on the local sunset and Panchang calculations. For 23 October 2026, the New Delhi timing is stated as 5:44 p.m. to 8:16 p.m., but devotees in other cities should confirm the timing through their local Panchang.
