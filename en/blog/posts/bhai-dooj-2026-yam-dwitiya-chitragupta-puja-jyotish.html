Bhai Dooj 2026: Yam Dwitiya, Chitragupta Puja and the Astrological Message of a New Beginning in Relationships

Bhai Dooj, also known as Yam Dwitiya, will be celebrated on 11 November 2026. Learn about the religious significance of the day, the puja procedure, astrological beliefs related to sibling relationships and important precautions.

Bhai Dooj will be celebrated in India on Wednesday, 11 November 2026. It is also known as Yam Dwitiya, Bhatri Dwitiya and, in several traditions, Chitragupta Puja. The festival is observed on the Dwitiya tithi of the Shukla Paksha after Diwali and is considered a symbol of affection, respect, protection and family responsibility between brothers and sisters.

According to the Delhi Panchang, Kartik Shukla Dwitiya will prevail on 11 November. However, the beginning and ending time of the tithi may vary by location, so it is appropriate to give priority to the local Panchang while planning puja or travel. On Bhai Dooj, a sister applies a tilak to her brother, performs an aarti and prays for his happiness and good health. The brother, in turn, makes a commitment to respect, support and protect his sister.

The Religious Story of Bhai Dooj and Yam Dwitiya

According to a mythological belief, Lord Yama and Yamuna were the son and daughter of Surya Dev and Sanjna. They shared a deep bond of affection. Yamuna invited her brother to visit her home several times. One day, Yama arrived at his sister’s house. Yamuna welcomed him, applied a tilak, performed an aarti and served him a meal. Pleased with her, Yama granted her a boon that a brother who visited his sister’s home on this day, accepted her hospitality with respect and received her blessings would be blessed with longevity and auspiciousness.

This story is traditionally associated with Yam Dwitiya. Its meaning is not limited to praying for a long life. The festival gives brothers and sisters an opportunity to revive communication, gratitude and responsibility in their relationship. In families where siblings live far apart, this feeling can also be expressed through a video call, a message or a small gift.

The Significance of Chitragupta Puja

Chitragupta Puja is also observed by several communities on Bhai Dooj. Chitragupta is regarded as the deity who maintains a record of people’s actions. The day is especially significant in the Kayastha community, but its message of karma, honesty and self-evaluation is useful for everyone. On this occasion, people worship account books, writing materials, workplaces and equipment connected with study or professional work.

From an astrological perspective, Chitragupta Puja encourages a person to review their actions. It is a good opportunity to consider how we have used our money, time, words and relationships during the year. If, along with the puja, a person prepares a list of pending tasks, accepts mistakes and sets clear goals for the period ahead, the spiritual meaning of the festival can become more meaningful.

A Simple Puja Procedure for Bhai Dooj 2026

  • Prepare a clean space for the puja and remember Lord Ganesha, Lord Yama and Chitragupta.
  • The sister should apply a tilak of roli, kumkum or sandalwood to her brother’s forehead. Akshat may be used according to the family tradition.
  • Perform an aarti for the brother and pray for his health, wisdom, success and longevity.
  • The brother should give his sister a gift and pledge to respect and support her.
  • According to one’s capacity, offer food, clothing, educational materials or financial assistance to someone in need.
  • Those observing Chitragupta Puja may organise and worship their writing materials, documents, account books and other work-related items.

It is not appropriate to regard one specific time as a universally auspicious muhurta for the entire country on this day. The puja time may differ because of sunrise, the tithi, location and local tradition. If a family wishes to perform the puja at a particular muhurta, it should confirm the timing through a reliable Panchang for its city or with a local priest.

Astrological Indications for the Sibling Relationship

In Vedic astrology, matters related to younger siblings and courage are generally examined through the third house. The lord of the third house, planets placed in it, the position of Mars and the relationships formed with these factors may indicate communication, cooperation and occasional disagreements with siblings. However, a relationship should not be judged by looking at only one house or one planet. The complete birth chart, dasha, transits and real-life circumstances must be considered together.

Bhai Dooj encourages people to view astrological indications not through fear, but as an inspiration to improve relationships. If brothers and sisters have been distant for a long time, the most effective remedy may be an honest conversation, a calm discussion about an old dispute and respect for one another’s boundaries in the future. Astrological remedies are useful only when they are accompanied by positive changes in behaviour.

In the context of Bhai Dooj, Mercury’s indication is especially useful in reminding us that relationships require not only affection but also clear and respectful communication.

Things to Avoid on Bhai Dooj

Do not let the tilak or puja become merely a formality. Harsh words, bringing old disputes into public view, unnecessary spending for display and comparing gifts can weaken the spirit of the festival. If a brother or sister cannot be present because of personal circumstances, understanding that situation is also part of respecting the relationship. Affection, trust and regular support are more important than the price of a gift.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. On which day will Bhai Dooj 2026 be celebrated?

Bhai Dooj 2026 will be celebrated on Wednesday, 11 November. It is known as Kartik Shukla Dwitiya and Yam Dwitiya. The tithi and puja time may vary slightly according to the local Panchang.

2. Is it necessary for the brother to visit his sister’s home on Bhai Dooj?

Traditionally, it is considered auspicious for the brother to visit his sister’s home. However, if this is not possible because of distance, health or other circumstances, affection and blessings can also be exchanged through a phone call, video call, message or another medium. The feeling behind the tradition is most important.
