26 October 2026: Ashwin Purnima, Valmiki Jayanti and Today's Astrological Guidance

On 26 October 2026, Ashwin Shukla Purnima, Valmiki Jayanti and the Monday fast coincide. Learn about the spiritual significance of the date, the influence of lunar energy and simple, balanced remedies.

Religious and Astrological Significance of 26 October 2026

Today, Monday, 26 October 2026, is Ashwin Shukla Paksha Purnima according to the Indian Panchang. The local Panchang for Delhi places the Purnima tithi at approximately 9:41 a.m. on this day. Valmiki Jayanti is also observed, and in some traditions, Meera Bai Jayanti is remembered as well. Since the day falls on a Monday, the importance of Shiva worship and Monday spiritual practices becomes greater.

Purnima is associated with the fullness of the Moon, emotional sensitivity, self-reflection and mental clarity. However, tithi timings, moonrise and calculations related to fasting and worship may vary slightly from one city to another. Therefore, for any specific puja or fast, it is advisable to consult the local Panchang for your city.

In many regions, Ashwin Purnima is also associated with Sharad Purnima or Kojagari Purnima, but the date of worship and nighttime traditions depend on the regional Panchang. This year, some Panchangs connect its observance with the night of 25 October, while 26 October clearly records the Ashwin Purnima tithi and other religious occasions. Therefore, without local calculations, one particular timing should not be treated as standard for the whole of India.

Inspiration from Valmiki Jayanti

Maharishi Valmiki is honoured as the Adikavi, or the first poet, and as the author of the Ramayana. His life journey conveys the message that a person is not permanently bound by the past. Through self-awareness, discipline, good company and knowledge, the direction of life can be changed.

Rather than limiting this day to religious rituals alone, it can be used to make a resolution for self-improvement. One may begin a regular study routine, review negative habits or try to improve communication within the family. The essence of Valmiki Jayanti lies in inner transformation more than in external identity.

  • Read a short passage from the Ramayana, the Gita or another inspirational text.
  • Donate books, notebooks or study materials to a student.
  • Identify a habit in your behaviour that needs improvement.
  • Help someone in need without seeking recognition.

Lunar Energy on the Full Moon and Its Effect on the Mind

In astrological tradition, the Moon is regarded as the significator of the mind, emotions, memory, motherhood, sleep and mental adaptability. Around the Full Moon, many people may experience their emotions more intensely. Some may feel more creative, social or spiritually engaged, while others may notice restlessness, changes in sleep or uncertainty while making decisions.

This should not be treated as a certain prediction. Every person's birth chart, planetary period, health, routine and present circumstances are different. Even so, today can provide a useful opportunity to observe your state of mind and pause briefly before reacting.

Sitting quietly for a few minutes at night and focusing on the breath, reducing mobile-phone use and drinking enough water can be simple yet useful practices. During emotional conversations, it may be especially helpful today to listen to the other person completely rather than drawing immediate conclusions.

Simple Ways to Worship and Practise Spirituality Today

The method of worship on Purnima may differ according to family and regional traditions. In general, after bathing in the morning, offer water to the Sun and remember your chosen deity. Since it is Monday, water may be offered to a Shiva lingam. If possible, chant the mantra “Om Namah Shivaya” with a calm mind.

After viewing the Moon at night, many families follow the tradition of offering water, akshat or white flowers. Follow your local tradition while offering the arghya, and do not expose the eyes directly to intense light. Those observing a fast should decide whether to fast only according to their age, health and medical advice.

The purpose of any remedy today should not be to create fear or claim certain benefits. Worship is a means of bringing discipline and calm to the mind. In cases of financial difficulty, health problems or serious relationship stress, do not depend only on astrological remedies; also seek practical assistance from a qualified professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is 26 October 2026 Sharad Purnima?

26 October is the Ashwin Shukla Purnima tithi. In some regions, the observance of Sharad Purnima or Kojagari Purnima may be connected with the night of 25 October. For worship and fasting, be sure to check the moonrise and tithi details in your city's Panchang.

2. Is it necessary to observe a Purnima fast today?

Observing a fast depends on personal faith and health. People with weak health, diabetes, pregnancy, regular medication or another medical condition should not fast without a doctor's advice. Fruit-based food, a sattvic meal or simply offering prayers may also be chosen according to one's tradition.

3. What simple remedy can be followed today?

Remember Shiva with a calm mind in the morning or evening, offer food or study materials to someone in need, and meditate for a few minutes at night. Do not treat the remedy as a guarantee of a specific financial, health or marital result; its purpose is to develop discipline and positivity in the mind.
