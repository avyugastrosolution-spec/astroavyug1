Vighnaraj Sankashti Chaturthi on 30 September 2026: Fast, Puja Vidhi and Simple Zodiac Remedies

Krishna Paksha Sankashti Chaturthi will be observed on 30 September 2026. Learn about the significance of Vighnaraj Sankashti, fasting rules, puja vidhi, moonrise precautions and simple Ganesha remedies according to the zodiac signs.

The fast of Krishna Paksha Sankashti Chaturthi will be observed on Wednesday, 30 September 2026. This tithi is considered dedicated to Lord Ganesha and, in many traditions, is known as Vighnaraj Sankashti Chaturthi. Sankashti represents a prayer for freedom from difficulties and obstacles. Therefore, this day is regarded as an opportunity to worship Ganesha for mental stability, relief from hurdles in one’s work and peace and happiness within the family.

According to Indian almanacs, Krishna Chaturthi will prevail on 30 September, although the time at which the tithi ends may vary slightly depending on the location. The Kolkata-based Positional Astronomy Centre, associated with the Government of India’s astronomical almanac, has also mentioned Krishna Chaturthi and Bharani Nakshatra for this day. Moonrise time differs from city to city, so it is advisable to check the local panchang before ending the fast. Spiritual Significance of Vighnaraj Sankashti Chaturthi

Lord Ganesha is worshipped as Vighnaharta, the remover of obstacles, and as the deity of wisdom and discernment. On Sankashti Chaturthi, devotees fast or observe a fruit-based diet during the day and offer prayers to Ganesha in the evening before offering arghya to the Moon. According to tradition, the fast is concluded after sighting the Moon and offering arghya. The purpose is not only to seek the fulfilment of wishes, but also to strengthen self-control, gratitude and wise judgment.

During the puja, devotees may meditate on the form of Lord Ganesha that grants the ability to make the right decisions amid life’s complications. For students, this day can represent concentration and discipline, while for those in jobs or business, it conveys the importance of planning one’s work and avoiding haste.

How to Observe the Sankashti Fast on 30 September

Morning Preparations

  • After bathing in the morning, wear clean clothes and take a vow to observe the fast before Lord Ganesha.
  • Place an idol or picture of Lord Ganesha in the puja area.
  • According to your capacity, decide whether you will observe a waterless fast, a fruit-based fast or a sattvic-food fast.
  • If you have a health condition, are pregnant, elderly or taking regular medication, do not observe a strict fast.

Simple Ganesha Puja Vidhi

  • Offer water, akshat, durva grass, red flowers and sindoor to Lord Ganesha.
  • Offer modak, jaggery, banana or any sattvic offering according to your means.
  • Chant the mantra “Om Gam Ganapataye Namah.” Devotion and concentration are more important than the number of repetitions.
  • You may recite the Ganesha Atharvashirsha, Ganesha Chalisa or a brief Ganesha prayer.
  • At the end of the puja, pray for the welfare of your family, society and all living beings.

Offering Arghya to the Moon and Concluding the Fast

On Sankashti Chaturthi, there is a tradition of offering arghya after sighting the Moon. Once the Moon becomes visible, arghya may be offered with water containing akshat, white flowers or a little durva grass. After this, perform Lord Ganesha’s aarti and break the fast with fruit-based or sattvic food. Moonrise time may differ in Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur, Bhopal, Kolkata and other cities. Therefore, the time for one city should not be treated as applicable to the whole of India. Things to Keep in Mind on This Day

  • Do not turn the fast into a competition. Follow rules according to your physical capacity.
  • Do not consider any expensive item or special ritual compulsory in the name of puja.
  • Avoid anger, harsh speech, lies and unnecessary arguments, as this is more in keeping with the spirit of Sankashti.
  • Give priority to the local panchang for the tithi and Moonrise time. The time shown in an online panchang may change when a different city is selected.
  • If you light a lamp in the puja room, keep it in a safe place and away from children and pets.

Simple Ganesha Remedies According to the Zodiac Signs

Zodiac-based remedies may be followed as practices of faith and self-reflection. They should not be treated as rules that guarantee definite results.

  • Aries and Scorpio: To control haste, offer red flowers to Lord Ganesha and sit quietly for a few minutes before making an important decision.
  • Taurus and Libra: For financial discipline, offer jaggery or modak and provide a meal to someone in need.
  • Gemini and Virgo: For clarity in studies, writing and communication, chant “Om Gam Ganapataye Namah” and prepare a list of unfinished tasks.
  • Cancer: For peace in the family, offer durva grass to Lord Ganesha and seek the blessings of an elder at home.
  • Leo: Resolve to avoid ego and unnecessary arguments, and contribute to an act of service after the puja.
  • Sagittarius and Pisces: Express gratitude towards a guru, teacher or spiritual guide, and read a religious or knowledge-enhancing book.
  • Capricorn and Aquarius: Begin a long-pending task by dividing it into small steps, and make a work-related resolution before Lord Ganesha.

Using the Day for Career, Relationships and Mental Peace

Instead of limiting Sankashti Chaturthi to worship alone, it can also be used as a day for practical improvement. If you are experiencing obstacles in your career, write down three priority tasks and complete one of them. If there is tension in a relationship, listen to the other person fully before responding. If you are worried about finances, review your expenses and prepare a simple plan for the next seven days. In astrological tradition, Ganesha worship is associated with wisdom and the removal of obstacles. However, in real life, outcomes also depend on your decisions, effort, communication and circumstances.

The most meaningful message of Sankashti Chaturthi is this: instead of running away from obstacles, overcome them with wisdom, patience and discipline.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Will Sankashti Chaturthi be observed on 30 September 2026?

Yes, Sankashti Chaturthi will be observed on 30 September 2026 because it falls on Krishna Paksha Chaturthi. Some almanacs also refer to it as Vighnaraj Sankashti. The tithi and Moonrise time may vary by city. ([drikpanchang.com](https://www.drikpanchang.com/panchang/day-panchang.html?geoname-id=1273724&utm_source=openai))

2. Can water be consumed during the Sankashti fast?

The rules of fasting depend on tradition, health and one’s personal resolution. Some people observe a waterless fast, while many devotees consume water, fruits or a sattvic fruit-based diet. If there is any health risk, do not observe a waterless fast and seek medical advice if necessary.

3. How should the fast be concluded if the Moon cannot be seen?

If the Moon cannot be seen because of clouds, city lights or local conditions, you may follow the Moonrise time given in the panchang for your area. You may also consider the practice followed by your family or local temple. Rather than fasting for an excessively long period, give priority to your health.
