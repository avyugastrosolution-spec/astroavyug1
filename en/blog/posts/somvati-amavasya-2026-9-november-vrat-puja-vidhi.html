Somvati Amavasya 2026: Importance of Fasting, Puja and Self-Reflection on November 9

Somvati Amavasya is being observed on Monday, November 9, 2026. Learn about the spiritual significance of the day, a simple puja procedure, charity, remembrance of ancestors and important precautions.

Somvati Amavasya 2026 is being observed today, Monday, November 9, 2026. An Amavasya that falls on a Monday is traditionally known as Somvati Amavasya. According to the Indian Panchang, the Amavasya tithi is present at sunrise today, which is why the day is considered significant for bathing, charity, worship of Lord Shiva and remembrance of ancestors. Spiritual Significance of Somvati Amavasya

Amavasya is regarded as the day when the Moon remains unseen. From an astrological and spiritual perspective, this period is considered suitable for self-reflection, mental peace and releasing old emotional burdens rather than focusing only on external activity. Monday is associated with Lord Shiva, which is why Shiva worship is given special importance on Somvati Amavasya.

According to popular traditions, married women observe a fast on this day for the health and longevity of their husbands. In many families, there is also a tradition of remembering ancestors, offering tarpan, feeding those in need and making donations. The purpose of these religious practices is not merely to seek a particular result, but also to strengthen gratitude, self-discipline and a sense of responsibility towards the family.

Ritual practices associated with Amavasya differ across regions. In some places, it is referred to as Kartik Amavasya, while the name of the lunar month may appear different because of the Amanta and Purnimanta calendar systems. This does not reduce the significance of the tithi, but local traditions and family beliefs should be respected.

How to Perform a Simple Puja Today

A very elaborate procedure is not necessary for Somvati Amavasya worship. Faith, cleanliness and self-restraint are given the greatest importance. Take a bath in the morning, wear clean clothes and clean the place of worship. Meditate on Lord Shiva, Goddess Parvati and your chosen deity.

  • Offer clean water to the Shiva lingam. If available, milk, bel leaves and white flowers may also be offered.
  • Chant the mantra “Om Namah Shivaya” with a calm mind. You may chant it 11, 21 or 108 times, according to your convenience.
  • Light a lamp and pray for the health, harmony and mental peace of the family.
  • Remember your ancestors and, in their name, donate food, clothing or useful items to someone in need.
  • If you are observing a fast, choose rules according to your physical capacity and health.

The worship and circumambulation of a peepal tree is also followed in some regions. If you observe this tradition, perform the ritual without causing harm to the tree. When tying a thread, do not wrap it tightly around the branches or trunk. Along with religious faith, protecting the environment is also important.

What to Keep in Mind During the Fast

A waterless fast on Somvati Amavasya is neither necessary nor suitable for everyone. Pregnant women, elderly people, children and those dealing with diabetes or other health conditions should not fast without medical advice. A fruit-based fast, light food or a simple sattvic diet may also be observed with devotion. The purpose of fasting is not to cause physical suffering, but to encourage discipline and positivity in the mind.

Try to avoid anger, arguments, intoxicants and abusive language throughout the day. Instead of creating unnecessary tension at home, communicate calmly. If possible, spend some time in meditation, prayer, religious reading or silence. Limiting digital distractions can also make the day’s self-reflection more meaningful.

Avoid making a show of charity. Food, grains, blankets, medicines, educational materials or other necessary items may be donated according to your means. Helping someone while preserving their dignity reflects the true spirit of charity.

Using the Day from an Astrological Perspective

In astrological tradition, Amavasya is associated with inner emotional depth, subconscious feelings and incomplete mental cycles. Rather than creating fear about the future or making major decisions hastily, today may be used for self-assessment. Making a list of the habits, relationships or worries you wish to let go of can be a practical step.

People dealing with career matters can review pending work today. Resolve to organise unnecessary expenses, a scattered routine and unfinished promises. If there is a misunderstanding in a relationship, begin a calm conversation instead of making accusations. Spiritual practice becomes truly meaningful only when its impact can also be seen in one’s behaviour.

What to Do and Avoid Today

  • Do: Take a bath, remember Lord Shiva, meditate, help those in need and respect the elders in the family.
  • Do: Pay attention both to cleaning the home and to clearing negativity from the mind.
  • Do not: Get expensive remedies or unnecessary rituals performed out of fear caused by superstition.
  • Do not: Cause suffering to an animal, tree or person in need in the name of a religious belief.
  • Do not: Follow a waterless fast or strict rules that may harm your health.

Conclusion

Somvati Amavasya on November 9, 2026, offers an opportunity for self-reflection, worship of Lord Shiva, gratitude and service. The importance of the day lies not only in a particular puja or remedy, but also in bringing balance to one’s thoughts and behaviour. A simple puja, a disciplined fast, remembrance of ancestors and assistance to those in need can make the day meaningful. Since timings may vary according to the local Panchang, give priority to the tithi and sunrise time for your city.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. When is Somvati Amavasya 2026?

Somvati Amavasya 2026 falls on Monday, November 9. According to timings for New Delhi, the Amavasya tithi began at approximately 11:28 a.m. on November 8 and will continue until around 12:32 p.m. on November 9. The timing may vary slightly depending on the city.

2. Is a waterless fast necessary on Somvati Amavasya?

No. A waterless fast is not mandatory. A person may observe the fast with fruit-based food or a sattvic diet according to their age, health and capacity. If you have a health condition, give priority to medical advice.

3. What can be donated on Somvati Amavasya?

According to your means, you may donate food, grains, clothing, blankets, medicines or educational materials. Charity should not be performed for show, but with the intention of providing genuine help to someone in need.
