Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2026: Lord Vishnu’s Awakening Festival, Puja Vidhi and Simple Zodiac Remedies

Dev Uthani or Prabodhini Ekadashi will be observed on November 20, 2026. Learn about its religious significance, puja vidhi, fasting rules, Parana timing and useful zodiac-based remedies.

Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2026: Why Is Today Special?

Today, Friday, November 20, 2026, Dev Uthani Ekadashi is being observed. It is also known as Prabodhini Ekadashi and Hariprabodhini Ekadashi. According to the Hindu calendar, it falls on the Ekadashi of the Shukla Paksha in the month of Kartik. This date symbolically marks the awakening of Lord Vishnu from His four-month period of yogic sleep. From this day onward, many families and traditions consider a new cycle of auspicious activities, wedding preparations and religious ceremonies to begin.

According to panchangs prepared for New Delhi, the Ekadashi tithi is effective around sunrise on November 20. However, the tithi, sunrise and Parana timings may vary depending on the city and the local panchang tradition. Devotees observing the fast should therefore give priority to the panchang of their area or the schedule followed by a nearby temple.

The spiritual message of Dev Uthani Ekadashi is not limited to worship or fasting. It also offers an opportunity for self-reflection, discipline, restraint and the thoughtful resumption of tasks that have remained pending for a long time. The day can be useful for calming the mind, bringing clarity to relationships and approaching responsibilities with renewed enthusiasm.

Religious Significance of Dev Uthani Ekadashi

According to Puranic tradition, Lord Vishnu remains in yogic sleep from Devshayani Ekadashi, the Ekadashi of the Shukla Paksha in Ashadha, until Kartik Shukla Ekadashi. This period is known as Chaturmas. The awakening of Lord Vishnu on Dev Uthani Ekadashi is considered to mark the conclusion of Chaturmas. In many regions, Tulsi and Shaligram are ceremonially married on this occasion, a tradition known as Tulsi Vivah. In 2026, some panchangs observe Tulsi Vivah on November 21, so devotees may worship Lord Vishnu today and prepare for Tulsi-Shaligram worship the following day.

It is also important to remember that the dates of Dev Uthani Ekadashi and Tulsi Vivah are not identical in every region. Differences may arise because of Amanta and Purnimanta month calculations, local sunrise timings and Vaishnava traditions. While observing religious customs, respecting the traditions followed by one’s family is the most appropriate approach.

Simple Puja Vidhi and Fasting Rules

After bathing in the morning, wear clean clothes, light a lamp before Lord Vishnu and take a resolve to observe the fast. Yellow flowers, Tulsi leaves, sandalwood, fruits and an offering may be presented during the puja. Chant the mantra “Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya” according to your capacity. Vishnu Sahasranama, verses from the Bhagavad Gita or hymns dedicated to Lord Vishnu may also be recited.

  • Those observing the fast should choose between a waterless fast, a fruit-based fast or a sattvic diet according to their health condition.
  • Traditionally, avoiding grains, non-vegetarian food, alcohol and excessively tamasic food is considered auspicious.
  • Staying away from anger, harsh speech, criticism and unnecessary arguments is in keeping with the spirit of the fast.
  • Donate food, clothing or a useful item to a person in need.
  • Break the fast on the following day during the Dwadashi tithi, according to the timing provided by the local panchang.

A panchang published for New Delhi gives the Parana time on November 21, 2026, as 1:11 p.m. to 3:18 p.m. This timing should not be considered the same for every city. Check the timing applicable to your location before breaking the fast.

The Day’s Message for Career, Marriage and Relationships

In many traditions, Dev Uthani Ekadashi is associated with the resumption of auspicious activities. However, decisions related to marriage, property, employment or major investments should not be based solely on the festival. For marriage, give importance not only to horoscope matching and the assessment of planetary combinations, but also to health, temperament, financial understanding and mutual consent. In career matters, today may be a good opportunity to create a new plan, reconnect with older contacts and set a schedule for skill development.

In relationships, the day encourages clear and respectful conversation instead of expanding a list of complaints. If a misunderstanding has continued for a long time, listen to the other person rather than reaching an immediate conclusion. Spirituality brings its real benefit only when patience and compassion are reflected in daily conduct along with worship.

What to Do and What to Avoid Today

Today, you may clean the home, organize the place of worship, light lamps, care for the Tulsi plant and prepare sattvic food. Reduce unnecessary time spent on mobile phones and social media, and make time for family, study or meditation. Do not pressure anyone to fast, especially children, elderly people, pregnant women or those with health concerns.

Health is the most important consideration during an Ekadashi fast. People with diabetes, blood pressure concerns, medication requirements or other medical conditions should consult a doctor before fasting. Remaining hungry is not an essential condition of religious practice; faith, restraint and good conduct are also important parts of this festival.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the Dev Uthani Ekadashi fast be observed on November 20, 2026?

According to the available panchangs, Dev Uthani or Prabodhini Ekadashi falls on November 20, 2026. However, the determination of the tithi may vary according to the local sunrise and regional tradition. Observe the fast according to the panchang applicable to your city.

When should Dev Uthani Ekadashi Parana be performed?

Parana is performed during the Dwadashi tithi. A published schedule for New Delhi gives the time as 1:11 p.m. to 3:18 p.m. on November 21. The timing may differ in other cities, so consult the local panchang.

Is it auspicious to get married on Dev Uthani Ekadashi?

After this day, many traditions consider marriage-related auspicious activities to resume. However, the final marriage muhurta should be decided after considering the location, date, nakshatra and the horoscopes of both individuals. Do not finalize a wedding date solely on the basis of the festival.
