13 November 2026: Astrological Significance of Nagula Chavithi and Kartik Shukla Chaturthi

On 13 November 2026, Kartik Shukla Chaturthi, Nagula Chavithi and Mula Nakshatra will coincide. Learn about the worship method, precautions and astrological significance of the day.

Friday, 13 November 2026, will mark the Chaturthi Tithi of the Kartik Shukla Paksha. In South Indian traditions, particularly in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Karnataka, this day is considered important as Nagula Chavithi or Nag Chaturthi. The occasion is associated with remembering the serpent deities, respecting nature and praying for the well-being of the family. According to the New Delhi Panchang, Shukla Chaturthi will continue until approximately 8:42 p.m., after which Panchami Tithi will begin. Mula Nakshatra will continue until around 5:17 p.m., followed by Purva Ashadha Nakshatra. Timings may vary slightly depending on the local city. Religious and Cultural Meaning of Nagula Chavithi

Nagula Chavithi is associated with the worship of the serpent deities. In Indian tradition, snakes are regarded not merely as living beings, but also as symbols of the earth, water, fertility and the balance of nature. Serpent worship has held a special place in lives connected with fields, water sources and soil. Therefore, the spirit of this day is not limited to seeking a particular wish; it is also connected with expressing gratitude towards nature and all living beings.

Many families worship the serpent deities, Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati on this day. In some regions, milk, turmeric, flowers and offerings are presented before a serpent idol or at a serpent temple. However, feeding milk to live snakes is not considered appropriate, as it can harm their health. Using an idol, image or symbolic representation installed in a temple is a safer and more responsible way to perform the worship.

Kartik Shukla Chaturthi and Ganesha Worship

Chaturthi Tithi is also associated with Lord Ganesha. Therefore, remembering Lord Ganesha along with the serpent deities on Nagula Chavithi may be considered an auspicious practice. The central purpose of Ganesha worship is to cultivate wisdom, discernment and the ability to deal with obstacles. On this day, devotees may clean the home, light a lamp, chant a Ganesha mantra and resolve to take simple, sattvic food.

During worship, chant “Om Gam Ganapataye Namah” or another Ganesha mantra according to your faith. “Om Namah Shivaya” may also be chanted for the serpent deities. More important than the number of repetitions are purity of intention, self-control and a commitment to non-violence. If a family is observing a fast, individual health, age and medical advice must be taken into account.

Influence of Mula Nakshatra on the Day

New Delhi Panchangs indicate that Mula Nakshatra will continue until the evening on 13 November. In astrological tradition, Mula Nakshatra is associated with roots, underlying causes and depth. The day may be considered useful for activities that require understanding the actual cause of a problem, organising old burdens and reviewing unnecessary habits. The presence of Mula Nakshatra does not mean that all new activities should be avoided throughout the day. For any auspicious or important decision, it is more appropriate to consider the birth chart, location, timing and nature of the activity together. In general, research, self-reflection and clear planning may be given priority over haste. After Purva Ashadha Nakshatra begins in the evening, it may be useful to focus on matters connected with confidence, goals and long-term efforts.

Simple Worship Method for the Day

  • Take a bath in the morning, clean the place of worship and light a lamp.
  • Meditate on Lord Ganesha and the serpent deities. If a serpent idol is unavailable, use an image or perform symbolic worship at a temple.
  • Offer turmeric, unbroken rice, flowers, water and fruit. Use milk only in limited quantity for an idol or Shiva Lingam; do not feed milk to live snakes.
  • Chant “Om Gam Ganapataye Namah” and “Om Namah Shivaya” with devotion.
  • Pray for peace and health within the family and for the protection of nature.
  • If possible, donate food, fruit or essential items to someone in need.

Those observing a fast may take fruits or sattvic food. A waterless fast is not necessary for everyone. People with diabetes, blood-pressure concerns, pregnancy, ongoing medication or any other health condition should preferably seek medical advice before fasting. The purpose of worship is not to cause physical suffering, but to cultivate discipline and gratitude in the mind.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Nagula Chavithi be observed throughout India on 13 November 2026?

Nagula Chavithi is more widely observed in South Indian and certain regional traditions. Its name, worship method and significance may differ from one state to another. Give priority to your family tradition and the Panchang followed by your local temple.

Is it necessary to offer milk to the serpent deity on this day?

No. Water, flowers, turmeric and offerings may be presented before an idol or image. Feeding milk to live snakes is not appropriate from a health perspective. Protecting wildlife while expressing devotion is also part of the spirit of the worship.

Can auspicious activities be performed during Mula Nakshatra?

Astrological traditions differ in their interpretation of Mula Nakshatra. A Nakshatra alone is not sufficient to determine whether an activity is auspicious or inauspicious; Tithi, weekday, ascendant, the nature of the activity and the individual birth chart should also be considered. For an important decision, seek personal guidance from a qualified astrologer.
