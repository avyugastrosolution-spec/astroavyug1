23 November 2026: Significance of Shiva-Vishnu Worship on Vaikuntha Chaturdashi and Kartik Monday

23 November 2026 brings together Kartik Shukla Chaturdashi and Monday. Learn about the significance of Vaikuntha Chaturdashi, the worship method, simple remedies and important precautions.

On Monday, 23 November 2026, Kartik Shukla Chaturdashi will occur in India. According to the New Delhi panchang, the Chaturdashi tithi will continue until approximately 11:42 p.m., after which the Purnima tithi will begin. Bharani Nakshatra and Variyan Yoga will remain in effect throughout the day. Several panchangs also mention Vaikuntha Chaturdashi, Kartik bathing and Kartik Chaumasi Chaudas on this date. (Source: Drik Panchang)

Vaikuntha Chaturdashi is considered especially significant for the joint worship of Lord Vishnu and Lord Shiva. It is traditionally believed that worshipping Hari and Har on Kartik Shukla Chaturdashi helps develop balance, self-restraint and spiritual concentration within the devotee. However, the rituals and significance of this day may differ across regions and traditions. It is therefore advisable to give priority to the customs followed by your local temple, family and city panchang.

The Spiritual Meaning of Vaikuntha Chaturdashi

In Hindu tradition, Lord Vishnu is regarded as the symbol of preservation and order, while Lord Shiva is associated with transformation, detachment and inner reflection. The message of Vaikuntha Chaturdashi is that both stability and change are necessary in life. External success alone is not enough; inner peace, discipline and the ability to make sound decisions are equally important.

The month of Kartik is considered a time for offering lamps, bathing, chanting and following a sattvic way of life. The purpose of worship on this day should not be limited to seeking a particular outcome. It should also encourage humility, service and self-control in daily conduct. If a complete ritual is not possible, one may light a lamp at home and peacefully chant the Vishnu Sahasranama, a Vishnu mantra or the Mahamrityunjaya Mantra.

How to Worship on 23 November

Morning Preparation

Wake up before sunrise and bathe. If bathing in a river or another sacred water body is not possible, a little Ganga water may be added to the water at home. Then wear clean clothes and clean the place of worship. Those observing a fast should make their resolve according to their health. People with weak health, pregnant women, elderly individuals and those taking regular medicines should not undertake a strict fast.

Worship of Lord Vishnu

Offer yellow flowers, Tulsi leaves, sandalwood, incense and a lamp to Lord Vishnu. Chant “Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya” according to your faith and ability. While offering Tulsi leaves, ensure that they are clean and presented with devotion. If possible, recite the Vishnu Sahasranama. Otherwise, sitting quietly for a few minutes while meditating on Lord Vishnu can also be a meaningful spiritual practice.

Worship of Lord Shiva

Offer clean water to the Shivling. If available, Panchamrit or milk may also be used, although water abhishekam alone is considered sufficient. Offer Bilva leaves, white flowers and incense while chanting “Om Namah Shivaya.” Follow the tradition of your local temple regarding the materials that may be offered to the Shivling. After the worship, offering food, clothing or useful items to someone in need can connect the day’s spiritual practice with service.

Lamp Offering and Evening Practice

In the evening, light a sesame-oil or ghee lamp at the place of worship, near the main entrance or in another safe location. If the lamp is placed outside, keep it where there is no danger of accidents caused by wind, children or pets. Offering a lamp is not only a prayer for prosperity; it also represents a resolve to reduce ignorance, fear and negative habits.

Since the day is Monday, Shiva mantra chanting may be included in the evening practice. Those who wish to worship both Vishnu and Shiva may first remember Lord Ganesha, then worship Vishnu followed by Shiva. Finally, pray for peace, health and wisdom for the family.

The Day’s Message for Career and Relationships

Kartik Shukla Chaturdashi can be a useful opportunity for self-reflection. If a task is stalled in your career, divide it into smaller steps and prepare a plan. Avoid decisions such as changing jobs, making a major investment or accusing someone without having complete information. Because Monday may bring emotional sensitivity, it is helpful to avoid harsh words during conversations.

In relationships, the day conveys a message of forgiveness, clear communication and taking responsibility. If you have a complaint against your spouse or a family member, express it calmly instead of using taunts. For unmarried people, this may be a time to build self-confidence and make their expectations more realistic. Decisions regarding marriage or relationships should not be based only on a date or zodiac sign. Nature, values, communication and practical compatibility should also be considered.

What to Avoid

  • Do not make expensive donations or unnecessary purchases merely for show.
  • Do not neglect your health in the name of fasting.
  • Be cautious of people who frighten others and sell worship services, doshas or expensive remedies.
  • Do not assume that one fixed auspicious time applies to every city without checking the local panchang.
  • Do not use materials during worship that may harm animals, birds or the environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a fast be observed for Vaikuntha Chaturdashi on 23 November 2026?

According to the New Delhi panchang calculation, 23 November 2026 is Kartik Shukla Chaturdashi, and several panchangs record it as Vaikuntha Chaturdashi. Before observing a fast, check the date for your city and your family tradition, as the time at which the tithi ends may vary by location.

Should both Shiva and Vishnu be worshipped on this day?

The tradition of jointly worshipping both deities is followed in many regions, but it is not compulsory. According to one’s faith, worship of only Shiva or Vishnu, mantra chanting, lamp offerings and service may also be performed. The value of worship is connected with devotion, self-restraint and good conduct.

Does any special remedy on Vaikuntha Chaturdashi guarantee results related to a job or marriage?

No astrological or religious remedy can provide a definite guarantee regarding employment, marriage or wealth. Treat worship and charity as support for mental discipline and positive effort. In career matters, skills and preparation are more important, while communication and respect should be prioritised in relationships.
