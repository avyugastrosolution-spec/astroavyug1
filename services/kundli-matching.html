✦ What is Kundli Matching?

Kundli Matching, traditionally known as Kundli Milan or Guna Milan, is a Vedic Astrology method used to study compatibility between two birth charts in the context of marriage.

The analysis traditionally considers the Moon signs, Nakshatras and other planetary factors represented in the two Kundlis.

At Astro Avyug, Kundli Matching is approached as a broader compatibility analysis rather than focusing only on a single score.

✦ Why Look Beyond Guna Score?

Traditional Guna Milan provides a structured compatibility framework, but a complete Kundli Matching consultation may involve additional factors.

Depending on the question, the analysis can also consider planetary placements, relevant houses, Moon signs, Nakshatras and other traditional Jyotish factors.

This broader approach provides more context than relying only on the total Guna score.

✦ How Kundli Matching Works

The consultation begins with the birth details of both individuals. The relevant birth charts can then be studied together.

Traditional compatibility factors are explained along with other relevant astrological observations according to the subject of the consultation.

The purpose is to provide information and perspective for thoughtful discussion about compatibility and marriage-related questions.

✦ Personalized Compatibility Analysis

Every pair of birth charts is different. Therefore, a personalized analysis can consider the specific planetary patterns present in both Kundlis.

Kundli Matching can be used as one traditional source of information alongside communication, mutual understanding, personal values and practical considerations in a relationship.
