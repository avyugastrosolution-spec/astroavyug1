🃏 Tarot & Reflection

Tarot can be useful as a reflective practice because the images and symbols can encourage you to consider a situation from another angle. Different traditions use different spreads and interpretations.

A reading should not be treated as a guaranteed prediction or as a replacement for medical, legal or financial advice. Important decisions should also consider reliable information, practical circumstances and your own judgment.

Note: Tarot is an intuitive and reflective practice. It does not guarantee a particular future event, relationship outcome, job, financial result or other specific outcome.
