✦ What is Vedic Astrology?

Vedic Astrology, also known as Jyotish, is a traditional Indian system that studies planetary positions, zodiac signs, houses and other astrological factors represented in a person's birth chart.

A birth chart, commonly known as a Kundli, provides a framework for studying different areas of life. Vedic Astrology can be used to explore themes related to career, relationships, marriage, education, finances, family and personal development.

At Astro Avyug, these concepts are explained in a practical and easy-to-understand manner, keeping the individual's question at the center of the consultation.
