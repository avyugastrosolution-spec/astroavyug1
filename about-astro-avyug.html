About Astro Avyug

Our Approach

Every consultation is approached with attention to the individual's questions and circumstances. Vedic Astrology, Kundli analysis, Tarot and Numerology are used as tools for guidance, reflection and better understanding.

The focus is on clear explanations, traditional knowledge and practical guidance rather than complicated terminology.
