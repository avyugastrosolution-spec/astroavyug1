✦ About Astro Avyug

Astro Avyug is a platform for personalized astrology and spiritual guidance, bringing together traditional Vedic knowledge with a modern and easy-to-understand approach.

The purpose is to help people explore their questions through Vedic Astrology, Kundli analysis, Tarot, Numerology and related spiritual guidance.

Every consultation focuses on understanding the individual's question and providing clear, thoughtful and practical guidance based on the selected service.

✦ Our Approach

Astrology is a traditional system of understanding planetary patterns, timing and different areas of life. At Astro Avyug, these traditional concepts are explained in a simple and accessible way.

The goal is to provide meaningful insights that can help you reflect on your situation and understand different possibilities.
